Wiring Notes

VCC — Use 3.3V, not 5V The UN2572 controller and its onboard LDO (RDMSY6) are 3.3V devices. Feeding 5V to the VCC pad will likely damage the IC. Use the 3.3V pin on P3 for display power.
TFT_DET — Tie directly to GND This pad is active-low. Holding it LOW permanently keeps the module awake. No GPIO pin needed — just run a short wire from TFT_DET → GND on the display board itself.
LV1 + LV2 — Bridge together The display has one data pad (U_TX.RX) but the ATmega needs separate TX and RX lines. Both LV1 and LV2 connect to the same pad. Twist or solder the two wires together before connecting to U_TX.RX.
TX vs RX — Does swapping matter? Pin 2 = TX (sends), Pin 3 = RX (receives). Since both land on the same physical pad, swapping the dupont wires on HV1/HV2 makes no difference to the circuit.
Level shifter power pins HV and LV are reference voltage pins, not signal channels. Both must be connected or the shifter won't work. HV → P3 5V, LV → P3 3.3V.
ATmega GPIO is always 5V Even though P3 has a 3.3V power output pin, every GPIO pin (including pins 2 and 3) outputs 5V logic. The level shifter is required on the data line regardless.
